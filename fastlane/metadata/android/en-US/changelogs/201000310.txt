2.1.0-rc.1

Audiobooks
• Audible books can be exported as one .m4b file with chapter markers.
• A book whose named audio-track language differs from the catalogue is held for review; an unknown language never holds.
• A no-source verdict is a reviewable hold, with Search again and Open in Audible.

Downloads
• A relaunch resumes only the tasks a process kill left in flight; a cancelled task stays cancelled.
• Warning-completed tracks are counted and named apart from clean ones in the batch header.
• Media the download archive already lists is reported as already downloaded.

Fixes
• Playlist items stay pinned to the video they were enqueued from.
• Two held songs in one batch no longer share or hide each other's input fields.
• Ghost downloads finish into the chosen folder, and gallery links reach quick download again.
• A failed replacement keeps the previous file and completes with a warning.
