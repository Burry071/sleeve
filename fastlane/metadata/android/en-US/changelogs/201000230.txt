2.1.0-beta.3

Matching
• Matching now ranks the top three candidates with per-component reasons, offers a review sheet for ambiguous picks, and re-downloads only the swapped song.
• The flagged match badge reads "N% match · needs review" as a warning instead of a success.
• Audible searches are built from the catalogue title and authors instead of the narrator roll.
• Audiobook matches refuse uploads far shorter than the book instead of queueing trailers and excerpts.

Logging
• Each task's log records whether cookies were used.
