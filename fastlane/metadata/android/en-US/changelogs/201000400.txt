Audiobooks
• Audible books export as one .m4b file with chapter markers; a long URL in the task name no longer stops the export.
• A book that does not match its catalogue is held for review with a reason; verified split books download as checked parts.
• A no-source verdict offers Search again and Open in Audible.

Downloads
• Playlist batches live in their own tab with Stop and Resume across the batch, and a relaunch resumes only the tasks a process kill left in flight.
• Ghost downloads with "Save to SD card" land in the chosen folder without downloading the file twice.
• A download no longer fails on sites that refuse a request with no browser identity.

Settings
• The SD-card folder row shows the granted folder immediately after you pick it.
